= 1. Introduction and Goals

== Domain story

Pet Clinic is a veterinary clinic management system.
Clients register as *Owners* and bring their *Pets* to the clinic.
*Vets* conduct *Visits* with the pets.

For precise definitions of these terms, see the xref:{relrootdir}/doc/arc42/12-glossary.adoc[glossary].

== Stakeholders

|===
| Role | Concern
| Domain expert | Domain correctness, what the system does for clinic staff and owners
| Architect | Structural decisions, technology choices, risk
| Backend developer | Implementing within established conventions for backend applications
| Frontend developer | Implementing within established conventions for frontend applications
|===
